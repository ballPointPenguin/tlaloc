Cut-off low soaks South Texas as the western ridge starts migrating east

SPC's Marginal severe risk near Corpus Christi allows isolated damaging winds or a brief tornado from marginal supercells, one already observed southwest of the city, but poor lapse rates limit the threat. Nowhere else in the US faces severe weather. Strong Pacific cyclones bring rain, mountain snow and very gusty winds to the Pacific Northwest, easing today before another round Monday night.
