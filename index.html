Seasonally this is a textbook early-July setup: the Southwest ridge is the North American Monsoon anchor, with the sprawling thermal low over Nevada, Utah and Colorado and a slack gradient across the Desert Southwest supporting continued heat buildup and terrain-driven convection, while we sit squarely in the heart of Northern Hemisphere severe season on the Plains and the eastern US. On the tropical side it is quiet for the date: NHC expects no Atlantic development over the next seven days and only a 10 percent chance for a disorganized East Pacific wave, and the Air Mass RGB shows a uniformly moist but unorganized Gulf and Caribbean. An emerging El Niño tends to lean toward a less active Atlantic hurricane season via enhanced shear, which is consistent with today's benign tropics, though it is still early in the season and the signal will matter more as we head deeper into the summer and fall.
