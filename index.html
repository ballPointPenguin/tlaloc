The single story today is atmospheric blocking. That ridge-trough-ridge sandwich shuts down progressive downstream motion, so nothing today is in a hurry. The practical consequence is persistence: a slow-moving surface front trailing from a 1005-1006 mb low over the northern Plains becomes the organizing axis for repeated rounds of storms, while the western ridge parks over the interior and cranks up the heat.

Expect a multi-day, slowly southward-migrating severe-and-flood threat rather than a one-day event, with the heat only deepening underneath the amplifying West Coast ridge.
