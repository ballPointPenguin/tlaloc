Climate Context

Seasonally this is peak severe-weather season giving way to early Atlantic hurricane season, and both signatures are on today's map at once. The ENSO state is in transition: the ONI cooled to a weak La Nina-leaning -0.55 in late 2025 before warming back through neutral and turning slightly positive (0.48) by March-May 2026, marking an incipient shift toward El Nino. That early-El-Nino lean is consistent with a Gulf/Texas-focused tropical genesis like Potential Tropical Cyclone One this early in the season, though it is only June and one system is not a seasonal signal. Out West, the persistent ridge and stratospheric intrusion are sustaining triple-digit heat with no monsoon moisture recovery yet evident over the Intermountain West and southern High Plains — typical for mid-June, before the North American Monsoon's deeper surge into the Southwest later in the summer.
