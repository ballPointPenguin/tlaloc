Seasonally this is peak severe-weather climatology for the central and northern Plains, and today's slow-moving, training-convection regime fits the late-June shift toward nocturnal, MCS-driven heavy rain. ENSO is essentially neutral and trending the warm way: after a weak cool episode that bottomed near -0.55 in OND 2025, the ONI has recovered through spring 2026 to roughly +0.48 in MAM, so this pattern is unfolding under near-normal-to-slightly-warm Pacific forcing rather than a strong La Nina or El Nino signal. The dry, windy Great Basin and Four Corners fire weather likewise reads as a seasonal pre-monsoon signature, with the North American Monsoon not yet established enough to wet down the Southwest under this western-trough/eastern-ridge configuration.
