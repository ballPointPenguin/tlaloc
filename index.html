This is peak-summer North America running close to the seasonal script. The North American Monsoon is active, supplying the moisture streaming north under the Southwest ridge for diurnal high-terrain convection and today's southern Arizona severe/flood threat, while severe-weather season is being expressed as ridge-periphery, northern-stream storms along a wavy Canadian frontal zone rather than classic deep-Plains supercells.
