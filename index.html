Climate Context

This is peak-summer North America near the seasonal script. The North American Monsoon remains active, feeding daily high-terrain convection across the Southwest and Rockies, and severe weather is expressed as ridge-periphery, northern-stream and frontal storms rather than deep-Plains outbreaks. The ENSO backdrop is a decisively developing warm state: the ONI has climbed from weak La Niña (-0.61 in OND 2025) through neutral in early 2026 to a forecast +0.95 to +1.39 by late spring and summer, i.e. a developing moderate El Niño.
