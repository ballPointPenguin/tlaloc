Climate Context

This is peak-season North America running close to the seasonal script, with the Atlantic basin at its early-September climatological maximum, an active North American Monsoon feeding daily high-terrain and Great Basin convection, and severe weather expressing itself as ridge-periphery, northern-stream and frontal storms rather than deep-Plains outbreaks. The ENSO backdrop is a developing warm state: the ONI has climbed from weak La Niña in late 2025 to roughly +1.39C by May-July 2026, a transition toward El Niño. That configuration typically favors an active eastern Pacific while shear and dry air lean against the Atlantic, and the current tropics fit the split — a 90 percent development area south-southwest of Mexico and a separate area near Hawaii against a comparatively quieter Atlantic, where Dolly's remnants and the homegrown Gulf low carry the only near-term odds (the Gulf remaining capable of spinning up its own systems regardless of basin-wide shear).
