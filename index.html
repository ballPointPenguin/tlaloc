That configuration is essentially blocked, so the whole system is moving slowly — and where the trough's eastern flank overlaps a moist, unstable airmass, it is anchoring a multi-day corridor of severe weather and heavy rain from the High Plains through the Mississippi and Ohio Valleys to the East Coast.
