That is strong shear over modest buoyancy, and cool air at high elevation is the main limit on instability. A northern-tier shortwave follows early next week with cooler air behind it. It is paired with a Hudson Bay-Baffin trough and persistent negative anomalies off California and over the Southwest. WPC flags real uncertainty in how that Pacific energy evolves.
