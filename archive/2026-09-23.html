Genuine moist air is confined to the Southeast, Gulf and Atlantic. The one compact dry feature worth flagging is a narrow orange-red streamer curling cyclonically into northern Mexico on the western flank of the Southwest system — a credible dry intrusion feeding that developing shortwave, distinct from the broad ridge signal to its north.
