Southern Canada is quiet. The strongest gradients ring 970 hPa marine lows over the Gulf of Alaska and Labrador Sea.

That holds cold air far to the north and aims the Pacific jet at the West Coast.

Climate Context

A strong El Niño is in place. The Oceanic Niño Index reached +2.16 for July-September, up from -0.39 last winter. It is still rising, but more slowly each season. A cool season under strong El Niño typically favors an active southern storm track. Today's moisture-laden Southwest troughs fit that tendency, though they aren't proof of it. The Atlantic hurricane season is ending quietly, with no development expected for a week. The East Pacific has only a low-odds area far to the west. The CPC teleconnection indices haven't been updated since September 30, so they say nothing about today's pattern.
